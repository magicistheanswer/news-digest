Customers are bringing their own AI to the customer journey

CMSWire · Nov 25, 2026
core / cx – AI assistants & journey measurement

Dan Hartman describes "shadow CX": the growing share of service interactions that happens inside a customer's personal AI assistant, outside any channel a brand owns or measures. Roughly three in four consumers have already used a general-purpose assistant to resolve a brand issue, often because automated support feels like more effort. His advice is to measure resolution instead of containment, fix broken journeys before automating them, and keep policies and support content consistent so outside AI tools give correct answers.
