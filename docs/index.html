AI models need more data about biology, and OpenAI is paying to create it

MIT Technology Review · Sep 15, 2026
core / ai — AI & biotech data

Policy analyst Ruxandra Teslo has proposed bidding on the bankruptcy proceedings of failed biotech companies to recover their regulatory filings, manufacturing strategies and safety data - what she calls biotech's "lost archive" - and using it to train AI systems. The OpenAI Foundation is now funding the idea through a new "Public Data for Health" effort, betting that higher-quality, real-world biological datasets can make AI genuinely useful as a drug-development copilot rather than just a faster search tool.
