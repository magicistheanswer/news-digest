A Department of Defense budget request seeks $30.3 million over five years for a program called "Polygraph+" (also "Polygraph Next"), aiming to improve lie detection through AI- and machine-learning-based scoring algorithms combined with "standoff sensing" - reading physiological signals without attaching a device to the subject.
