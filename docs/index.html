Martech category labels don't mean anything anymore

MarTech.org · Sep 24, 2026
core / martech — DXP category churn

Gene De Libero pulled six years of homepage snapshots (2020–2026) for 23 digital experience platform vendors and found that most of them have repeatedly rebranded their own category: one vendor cycled through six different labels in six years (Jamstack, composable DXP, composition platform, visual workspace, plain DXP, agentic DXP), and only four of the 23 kept the same term the whole time. The takeaway is that a vendor's self-chosen category label now tracks whatever term is trending in martech marketing that year far more than it tracks any stable product identity - buyers evaluating a "DXP" or an "agentic platform" should look at what the product actually does, not what it currently calls itself.
