When can we say AI made a scientific discovery?

MIT Technology Review · Sep 28, 2026
core / ai — AI for science

Anthropic says its new molecular-biology lab, where Claude agents read literature and propose conjectures while human scientists run the experiments, has made its first discovery. The article probes that claim: after 21 hours, roughly 950 agents flagged a previously uncatalogued repeating pattern around a known enzyme - not a brand-new sequence - and a Copenhagen biologist says their team had already found it. The broader point is that what is novel for an AI system can still be routine or inconsequential to domain experts.
