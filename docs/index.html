IAB sets new standards for agentic buying and measurement

MarTech.org · Sep 22, 2026
core / martech — standards for agentic ad buying

IAB Tech Lab released AAMP 3.0, the latest version of its Agentic Advertising Management Protocols, adding a new OpenProposal specification meant to standardize the negotiation step between an advertiser's brief and an actual buy commitment - it's open for public comment through Oct. 22. Alongside it, IAB published a Measurement Services Addendum v1.0, a standard contractual template covering measurement, verification, attribution and analytics services, meant to save companies from negotiating every measurement agreement from scratch. Together the two releases try to give agentic ad-buying a common technical and legal footing as more of the transaction chain gets automated.
