Intuit sells Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for $3.68 billion

MarTech · Nov 19, 2026
core / martech – email, M&A

Bending Spoons, the Italian company known for buying software products and restructuring them hard (Evernote, Vimeo, WeTransfer), has agreed to buy Mailchimp from Intuit for $3.68 billion. That is less than a third of the $12 billion Intuit paid in 2021. Intuit gets about $3.31 billion in cash and $368 million in Bending Spoons stock. The deal still needs regulatory approval and is expected to close in early 2027. Mailchimp customers should expect pricing and product changes.

The brand, legal and regulatory exposure of auto-generated creative

MarTech · Nov 19, 2026
core / martech – AI governance, creative

This piece lists the risks that come with AI-generated assets. A brand voice can splinter across assets, copyright can be infringed through reference images or 'make it look like X' prompts, and an invented product claim counts as false advertising. Personal data put into prompts is still covered by GDPR and similar laws. The article stresses that liability depends on the facts of each case, not on one blanket rule. It recommends human review before publishing, logs of prompts and revisions, and governance shared between legal, security and brand teams. A good companion to yesterday's brand-governance piece.
