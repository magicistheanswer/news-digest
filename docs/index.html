An enterprise guide to zero-party, first-party and synthetic data

MarTech · Nov 20, 2026
core / martech – data strategy, privacy

This long guide sets out what each data type is good for and where it breaks. Zero-party data is limited by how much customers are willing to share, and what they say does not always match what they buy. First-party data powers things like churn models but still carries full privacy obligations and decays without upkeep. Synthetic data helps with AI training and testing privacy workflows, but it can copy and amplify the biases of its source data, so it needs checking against real behaviour.
